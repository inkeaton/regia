# Map: Regia 2.0 revision

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A decision for each of the 70 causes in `docs/guide/20 - Review List.md`: fixed in 2.0, kept as it is, or deferred. The decisions are carried into `docs/spec/`, `docs/adr/` and `GLOSSARY.md`, so that the spec handed to development is the revised one.

Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-13: a ticket closes when its spec chapters, ADRs and glossary entries are edited, not when the answer is agreed.

Widened by the user on 2026-11-17: each cause also gets whether it is in **Regia 1.5**, the subset of 2.0 built first (ADR 0025), and the spec says which parts are 1.5.

## Notes

- **One ticket per group of causes**, not per cause and not per `#revise`. Causes that the Review List cross-references are discussed together. Every cause (B1 to B16, L1 to L9, N1 to N15, F1 to F10, D1 to D20) is in exactly one ticket, except D1, D15, D16, F10 and L8, which are split by subject.
- **The Review List's five parts are a proposal.** A ticket may move a cause from one part to another.
- **Deliberate limits are confirmed or reopened inside the ticket of their subject.** There is no separate ticket for them.
- **Resolution**: one line per cause (fixed, kept, deferred), then the spec chapters, ADRs and glossary entries changed. A reopened ADR is superseded by a new one, not edited.
- **The guide is not kept in step here.** `docs/guide/` and its showcase describe the design before this revision. Updating them is under *Not yet specified*.
- Source material: the Review List for the causes, the guide chapters it names for the examples, `docs/guide/showcase/README.md` for *Spec wording* and *Gaps in the showcase*.
- Tickets: `.scratch/regia-revision/issues/NN-<slug>.md`, with `Type:`, `Status:`, `Assignee:` and `Blocked by:` lines. The frontier is the open, unblocked, unclaimed tickets, lowest number first.
- Skills: `grilling` and `domain-modeling` for every ticket.
- **Language or game design.** The language forbids or warns only about what can't be meant, or what the writer can't avoid. A game-design hazard (an Agent in two duels at once) is power the writer must use well, and the Trace shows it. Set by the user on 2026-11-17.
- **Playbooks decide, Plots nudge.** A Playbook describes what its Agent decides, from the events in the world and its own Goals: inside a Playbook, doing something is always the Agent's own choice. Decisions from outside (timeouts, the game and the Director pushing the story) live only in Plots, which nudge Agents towards the outcome. Set by the user on 2026-11-17.
- **1.5.** Every resolution ends with a *1.5* line: per cause, `in`, `later`, or in with what is left out. The systems in 1.5 are in [*Which systems Regia 1.5 keeps*](issues/21-which-systems-1-5-keeps.md). Every cause is still decided for 2.0 as a whole. While deciding, prefer answers where a 1.5 part doesn't need a later one, and say so in the ticket when it does.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per resolved ticket: [title](issues/NN-slug.md): gist -->

- [What passes between a Playbook and a Plot](issues/01-playbook-plot-interface.md): nothing is declared; the Transpiler infers each Interface and warns on mismatches, a Plot-local `GOAL` is an error, and an `EVENT` may be local to a Playbook.
- [A Goal's life: reached, dropped, repeated](issues/02-goal-lifecycle.md): every request is its own Pursuit; `REACHED` and `ENDED` join `DROPPED` and `FAILED`, and a Director hears them for a Goal it asked; a drop spreads upward as a drop; a recipe repeats with `PURSUE g, WITHOUT WAITING.`
- [Failure a Plan can read, and answers to an `ASK`](issues/03-failure-and-replies.md): `FAIL WITH` gives a Failure a Reason, read by `FAILED WITH`; whoever asked a Goal hears its endings, with `BY` naming who pursued it, in Playbooks and Plots; waiting on an `ASK` and refusing stay deferred, and ADR 0005 stands.
- [The Sender, the game and "me"](issues/04-sender-and-self.md): `FROM GAME` and `FROM ME` name what has no Sender, `FROM` takes `OR` alternatives, and `ME` is a Value for the Agent itself; no `FROM` still matches anything, and an own `PURSUE` never inherits a Sender.
- [When a Giving starts and ends, and what a joiner gets](issues/05-givings-and-joiners.md): `WHEN ASSIGNED:` and `WHEN UNASSIGNED:` in a Playbook, which still runs its closing Plans when taken; a Phase may carry Role setups and `UNTIL EXITED` ties a Giving or a Subplot to it; a Phase counts before its setups run; calling off an asked Goal is deferred.
- [What can be said about a Role](issues/06-roles.md): `PLAYS` is a condition that says who plays a Role, `CAST` is the Role every Plot has and may carry a setup, recipients take counts, a Plot's Role takes `EXCEPT`, and same names bind ignoring case; the pick per receiver is deferred.
- [The Vocabulary and the game](issues/07-vocabulary-and-the-game.md): `GAME FACT`/`GAME EVENT` mark what the game produces; External names are translated, a dotted Action is the Target's own code; Names are declared with `NAME`; types stay past 2.0.
- [Telling Stagings and Givings apart, and what a Subplot is given](issues/08-stagings-and-casts.md): a Plot takes arguments, and a parent tells Stagings apart by matching them; an Agent may play in two Stagings of one Plot; `UNASSIGN` for one Agent takes what was given the way it names; a Subplot still only adds, and casts below the Root stay fixed.
- [What a Director knows about itself, and how a Subplot's ending is heard](issues/09-director-state-and-endings.md): `IN <phase>`, `<Plot>(args) RUNNING` and `EXITED BY PARENT`; a Subplot's endings are heard like a Goal's (`ENDED WITH`, `ENDED WITHOUT OUTCOME`, `DROPPED`, then `ENDED`), an ending from above included; where a transition goes stays in the Plan that runs it; waiting for a Subplot is deferred.
- [Which systems Regia 1.5 keeps](issues/21-which-systems-1-5-keeps.md): all of 2.0 except `FROM GAME`/`FROM ME`/`OR`, `BY` and the asker hearing endings, Quota Triggers, and joining; leaving stays.
- [Quotas](issues/10-quotas.md): a Quota Plan reads what it counted with `?x SENT e(args)`, `?` doesn't split a count, a share rounds up everywhere, and the quantity is an Expression; firing every time, `ALL` of nobody and firing on a departure are kept; an `n OF` the build can never fill is a warning.
- [Facts that are told, and news in general](issues/11-told-facts.md): all kept; a told Fact is taken back by the receiver's own Plans (`WHEN UNASSIGNED: FORGET`), only its first teller is heard, a Director tidies Facts about a leaver on `LEFT`, Agents hear cast changes only through the Director, and ADR 0003 stands.
- [Which Plan runs](issues/12-which-plan-runs.md): ADR 0004 and ADR 0008 stand; falling through is one rule for every Trigger and stays implicit; name order stays with its warning; a default gives way where it would close a cycle (ADR 0032); `SELF` is a Plot's own Plans; F6 is deferred.
- [Labels and the Selection function](issues/13-labels-and-selection.md): choosing by Propensity stays VEsNA-Pro's (ADR 0033); an unlabelled candidate and a missing value are neutral (0.5 Trait, 0 Mood), so mixing is allowed; `SHIFT m BY e.` changes a Mood from a Body; Propensity code is emitted only where needed; a language-defined fit and when a Shift applies are deferred.
- [Numbers, rolls and repetition](issues/14-numbers-and-chance.md): a roll is whole unless a bound is a written decimal (ADR 0034), and a Propensity roll is always decimal; a name made in every Option outlives it; setups roll per Agent; the seed covers every draw; wrong Weights are Trace entries, not warnings; a weighted Pick `, WEIGHT e` and empty Options are added; loops stay deferred.
- [Agent kinds and Instancing files](issues/15-instancing.md): the Program says what holds for any cast, Instancing what holds for one cast before the run, the game the rest (ADR 0035); `KIND` with `LIKE`, line-by-line overriding and `NO KIND`; individual Agents (`AGENT gawain LIKE knight`) with setup lines and names usable as Values; `SEED`; Instancing comments gone; an unknown Target warns; Targets in lowercase.
- [The command line, Programs and Diagnostics](issues/16-command-line-and-diagnostics.md): `check`, `build`, `run`, `test` and `explain`, with `--target`, `--seed` and `--console`; one Entry per Program, and `ROOT` named, never inferred, so a `.regia` Entry is check-only (ADR 0036); Target checks run in `check`; `?_name` and `#@allow` answer warnings; two severities; the Build folder mirrors the Entry path and keeps `traces/`; the code shape kept for 2.0.
- [What the Trace records](issues/17-trace.md): what found no Plan is an `unheard` entry at info level with a code (`T3801`/`T3802` no longer warnings, ADR 0037); a `plan` entry names its Block and says how it beat its rivals; a Subplot's Director is always `director:<Plot>#<n>`; `mood` and `count` entries; game percepts stay unrecorded.
- [The Visual Editor](issues/18-visual-editor.md): `DURING PLOT` drawn by default; every file has a Docs page that shows its Interface, and only Plots are drawn; the Trigger picker is built from the Interface and declares a new Event where both sides reach it; a rename previews doc comments, the file and name-order flips; undo spans files; an Active Entry picks the Program shown; moving an Extent and Firefox/Safari disk folders are deferred past 2.0.
- [Time and Actions in the game](issues/19-time-and-actions.md): `WAIT d, UNTIL cond.` ends early and doesn't fail on time; `AFTER d:` is a Plot-only Phase timeout; the run's clock stops while the game is paused; a waiting `DO` whose Plan stops calls its Action off (ADR 0038); `EVERY`, `WITHIN` and the game's time scale stay deferred.
- [Words that do several jobs, and syntax nobody used](issues/20-words-and-syntax.md): no word gets a second spelling; `EVENT` becomes `CUE` and Event is anything that starts a Plan (ADR 0039); `.` and `;` stay optional, with Flat blocks closed by `;`; `PASS` fills an empty Block; a doc comment covers every item on its line; ADRs 0001, 0002 and 0009 stand.
- [The Regia 1.5 cut](issues/22-the-1-5-cut.md): every chapter ends in a `## Regia 1.5` section, indexed in chapter 27; a 1.5 build reports a later construct as `R0109` and refuses a spawn with `T1902`, both retired in 2.0; `#! regia:` marks cases that don't run on both; no version option.
