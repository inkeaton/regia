Action Specification &
Specification of an action, including any preconditions and post-conditions. &
\lstinline|ACTION| declaration &
Partial &
Names and typed parameter slots exist; no precondition or postcondition can be declared. \\
\hline

Agent Definition &
Specification of the initial state of an agent just after it is created. &
(none) &
None &
No per-agent initial-belief or initial-state declaration construct exists. \\
\hline

Environment Statement &
A Boolean statement about the environment. &
\lstinline|FACT| declaration / \lstinline|IF| condition &
Partial &
Regia Facts are agent-scoped beliefs; the concept is conflated with Belief rather than kept as a distinct environment statement. \\
\hline

Facet Action Specification &
Specification of a facet action in terms of the facet definition it will change and the new value it will write to the facet. &
\lstinline|WORLD DO BELIEVE(fact).| (Director self-belief write) &
Partial &
A Director-held belief write is structurally facet-like, since it produces state shared above any single Role-playing agent, but it is not a typed, declared facet write. \\
\hline

Facet Definition &
Specification of the structure of a given facet, including its name, data type and access mode. &
(none) &
None &
No declaration distinguishes a shared, environment-level fact from an ordinary agent \lstinline|FACT|, and no per-role sense or change permission exists. \\
\hline

Functional Requirement &
Requirement that provides added value to the users of the system. &
(none) &
None &
Regia has no requirements layer. \\
\hline

Interaction Protocol &
Specification of patterns of communications that occur in the system. &
(none) &
None &
\lstinline|TELL| and \lstinline|BROADCAST| exist as primitives, but no protocol or conversation structure can be declared. \\
\hline

Mental State Specification &
Specification of the initial mental state in terms of specified beliefs and agent goals. &
(none) &
None &
\lstinline|TEMPER| declares personality thresholds, not an initial belief or goal state. \\
\hline

Message Action Specification &
Specification of a message action in terms of the message schema and parameters to use. &
\lstinline|DO TELL(...).| / \lstinline|DO BROADCAST(...).| &
Full &
Direct match to the reserved \lstinline|TELL| and \lstinline|BROADCAST| primitives. \\
\hline

Message Schema &
Specification of the structure and semantics of a given kind of messages that can occur within the system. &
(none) &
None &
\lstinline|TELL| and \lstinline|BROADCAST| take untyped argument lists; no schema can be declared. \\
\hline

Non-Functional Requirement &
Requirement about any limits, constraints or impositions on the system to be built. &
(none) &
None &
Regia has no requirements layer. \\
\hline

Ontology &
Structural model of a given domain. &
(none) &
None &
The \lstinline|ACTION|, \lstinline|EVENT|, and \lstinline|FACT| vocabulary is flat and untyped; no domain ontology construct exists. \\
\hline

Organization Definition &
Specification of a collection of roles and agents co-operating towards a system goal. &
\lstinline|PLOT| header (\lstinline|PHASE| and \lstinline|ROLE| declarations) &
Full &
A Plot's declared \lstinline|ROLE| set is exactly a collection of agents co-operating toward the Plot. \\
\hline

Plan Resource Specification &
Specification of resources that are used in the Plan Specification. &
(none) &
None &
Regia has no resource concept. \\
\hline

Plan Specification &
An organized collection of action specifications. &
\lstinline|WHEN| block body (\lstinline|DO| statement sequence) &
Full &
A \lstinline|WHEN| body compiles to an ordered AgentSpeak plan body. \\
\hline

Policy &
A rule that specifies an arrangement of events expected to occur in a given environment. &
(none) &
None &
\lstinline|DURING| and \lstinline|TRANSITION| give structure to the Plot, but no first-class, declarative Policy object exists. \\
\hline

Requirement &
Feature that a system must implement. &
(none) &
None &
Regia has no requirements layer. \\
\hline

Resource Specification &
A resource specification specifies something that has a name, may have reasonable representations and that can be acquired, shared or produced. &
(none) &
None &
Regia has no resource concept. \\
\hline

Role &
Specification of a behavioural pattern expected from some agents in a given system. &
\lstinline|ROLE| declaration &
Full &
Direct match. \\
\hline

Role Compatibility &
Role relationship in which the source role is incompatible with the destination role for a given purpose. &
(none) &
None &
No inter-role relationship can be declared. \\
\hline

Role Dependency &
Role relationship in which the source role depends on the destination role for a given purpose. &
(none) &
None &
No inter-role relationship can be declared. \\
\hline

Role Relationship &
Social relationship between two roles for a given purpose. &
(none) &
None &
\lstinline|MAPPING| binds roles across a subplot call, but it does not declare a persistent relationship between them. \\
\hline

Service &
A single, coherent block of activity in which an agent will engage. &
\lstinline|PLAYBOOK| &
Partial &
Playbooks bundle coherent behaviour, but they are not advertised or discoverable as services. \\
\hline

System &
Final product of an agent-oriented software development project. &
\lstinline|PLOT| / whole \lstinline|.regia| program &
Partial &
Regia has no single System wrapper distinct from an individual Plot. \\
\hline

System Goal &
A specification of the state of the environment that the system tries to achieve. &
(none) &
None &
The \lstinline|IF| guard preceding a \lstinline|TRANSITION| weakly resembles a goal condition, but no first-class goal-of-the-system object exists. \\
\hline

Task &
Specification of a piece of behaviour that the system can perform. &
\lstinline|ACTION| declaration (conflated) &
Partial &
Regia draws no Task/Action distinction; both collapse into \lstinline|ACTION|. \\
\hline

\section{Default Benchmark Program}
\label{app:default-config}

The following listing reproduces the Regia program the benchmark generator of Section~\ref{sec:benchmark-tool} produces for the default configuration of Section~\ref{sec:benchmark-default}: 10 Actions, 10 Events, 5 Facts, 2 Roles, 2 Phases, and 2 Playbooks with 2 Plans each, 1 branch per Plan, and 2 statements per branch.

\begin{codeblock}[]{Regia}
# =======================================================
# Generated by Regia Benchmark Generator
# n_actions=10  n_events=10  n_facts=5
# n_playbooks=2  n_plans=2
# n_branches=1  n_stmts=2
# n_roles=2  n_phases=2
# n_subplot_breadth=0  n_subplot_depth=0
# seed=42
# =======================================================

# == Actions ==
ACTION act_0.
ACTION act_1.
ACTION act_2.
ACTION act_3.
ACTION act_4.
ACTION act_5.
ACTION act_6.
ACTION act_7.
ACTION act_8.
ACTION act_9.

# == Events ==
EVENT evt_0.
EVENT evt_1.
EVENT evt_2.
EVENT evt_3.
EVENT evt_4.
EVENT evt_5.
EVENT evt_6.
EVENT evt_7.
EVENT evt_8.
EVENT evt_9.

# == Facts ==
FACT fct_0.
FACT fct_1.
FACT fct_2.
FACT fct_3.
FACT fct_4.

# =======================================================
# Playbooks
# =======================================================

PLAYBOOK Playbook0:
    WHEN evt_0:
        IF fct_0:
            DO act_0.
            DO act_1.
        ELSE:
            DO act_0.
    WHEN evt_1:
        IF fct_1:
            DO act_2.
            DO act_3.
        ELSE:
            DO act_1.

PLAYBOOK Playbook1:
    WHEN evt_2:
        IF fct_2:
            DO act_4.
            DO act_5.
        ELSE:
            DO act_2.
    WHEN evt_3:
        IF fct_3:
            DO act_6.
            DO act_7.
        ELSE:
            DO act_3.

# =======================================================
# Main Plot
# =======================================================

PLOT MainPlot.

    PHASE phase_0 INITIAL.
    PHASE phase_1.

    ROLE Role0.
    ROLE Role1.

    DURING PLOT:
        WHEN evt_0:
            END PLOT.

    DURING phase_0:
        ON ENTER:
            ASSIGN Playbook0 TO Role0.
            WORLD DO act_0.
            ASSIGN Playbook1 TO Role1.
            WORLD DO act_1.

        ON EXIT:
            UNASSIGN Playbook0 FROM Role0.
            UNASSIGN Playbook1 FROM Role1.

        WHEN evt_1:
            TRANSITION TO phase_1.

    DURING phase_1:
        ON ENTER:
            ASSIGN Playbook0 TO Role0.
            WORLD DO act_0.
            ASSIGN Playbook1 TO Role1.
            WORLD DO act_1.

        ON EXIT:
            UNASSIGN Playbook0 FROM Role0.
            UNASSIGN Playbook1 FROM Role1.

        WHEN evt_2:
            END PLOT.
\end{codeblock}
